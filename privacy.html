🎅 Santa Is Really Sorry - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 22, 2024

Quick Summary: We only collect your phone number. We don't share it with anyone. We use it only to send you animal facts. You can unsubscribe anytime.

1. Introduction

Santa Is Really Sorry ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our SMS notification service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Phone Number: Your mobile phone number (provided when you're whitelisted)
  • SMS Messages: Any messages you send to our service (e.g., "HELLO" to opt-in, "STOP" to unsubscribe)
  • Opt-in Confirmation: Record of your initial opt-in message

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Subscription Date: When you first opted in
  • Message Delivery Status: Whether messages were successfully delivered
  • Subscription Status: Which animals you're subscribed to and which you've unsubscribed from
  • Message History: Count of facts sent (not content of your replies)
  • Timestamps: When messages were sent and received

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Your name (unless you provide it in a message)
  • ❌ Email address
  • ❌ Physical address
  • ❌ Payment information (service is free)
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Device information
  • ❌ Browsing history

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information ONLY for:

  • ✅ Sending animal fact messages to you
  • ✅ Managing your subscriptions and preferences
  • ✅ Processing unsubscribe requests
  • ✅ Responding to your support inquiries
  • ✅ Ensuring message delivery and service quality
  • ✅ Complying with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

4.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following services to operate our SMS program:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): For message delivery via Amazon SNS
    • AWS Privacy Policy: https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/
    • AWS stores your phone number and message data according to their privacy policy
  • Your Mobile Carrier: Must transmit messages to your device
    • Your carrier can see message content and metadata
    • Review your carrier's privacy policy for details

4.2 We Do NOT:

  • ❌ Sell your phone number to anyone
  • ❌ Share your information with marketers
  • ❌ Use your data for advertising
  • ❌ Share with social media platforms
  • ❌ Rent or lease your information

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or legal process.

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 Where Data is Stored

  • Data is stored in AWS databases (DynamoDB) in the US-East-1 (Virginia) region
  • AWS provides industry-standard security measures
  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest

5.2 How Long We Keep Data

  • Active subscribers: Data retained while service is active
  • After unsubscribe: Data retained for 30 days then deleted
  • Service termination: All data deleted within 90 days
  • Legal requirements: May retain longer if required by law

5.3 Security Measures

  • AWS IAM roles with least-privilege access
  • Encrypted database connections
  • Regular security updates
  • No public access to databases

6. Your Rights & Choices

6.1 Right to Unsubscribe

  • Unsubscribe from any animal at any time by replying with the appropriate keyword
  • See our Terms & Conditions for specific keywords
  • We will process your request immediately

6.2 Right to Access

  • Request a copy of your data by emailing support@santaisreallysorry.com
  • We'll provide your phone number, subscription dates, and message counts
  • Response within 30 days

6.3 Right to Deletion

  • Request complete deletion of your data
  • Email support@santaisreallysorry.com with "DELETE MY DATA"
  • We'll delete within 7 business days
  • You'll receive confirmation via SMS
h2>7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has subscribed, contact us immediately at support@santaisreallysorry.com and we will delete their information.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to say no to the sale of personal information (we don't sell)
  • Right to deletion
  • Right to equal service and price (even if you exercise privacy rights)

To exercise these rights, email support@santaisreallysorry.com

9. International Users

This service is operated in the United States. By subscribing, you consent to your information being transferred to and processed in the United States.

10. Do Not Track

Our service does not track your online activities. We only receive messages you directly send to our service number.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via SMS. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact Us

For privacy questions, concerns, or to exercise your rights:

  • Email: support@santaisreallysorry.com
  • Subject Line: "Privacy Request"
  • Response Time: Within 48 hours for urgent matters, 30 days for data requests

13. Consent

By subscribing to Santa Is Really Sorry, you:

  • ✅ Consent to this Privacy Policy
  • ✅ Consent to receiving SMS messages
  • ✅ Consent to data processing as described
  • ✅ Acknowledge you can withdraw consent by unsubscribing

Summary: We take your privacy seriously. We collect minimal data (just your phone number), use it only for sending you animal facts, don't share it with anyone (except AWS/carrier for delivery), and delete it when you unsubscribe. You have full control.
